Sign up and create your account. Make sure to use a referral code to unlock additional benefits.
💰 Step 2: Deposit Funds

🖼 Step 3: Purchase an NFT
Browse available NFTs on the dashboard and purchase one based on your budget.
📈 Step 4: Start Earning
Once you purchase an NFT, your daily earnings will automatically begin.
💸 Step 5: Withdraw Earnings
You can withdraw your earnings anytime from the withdrawal section.
